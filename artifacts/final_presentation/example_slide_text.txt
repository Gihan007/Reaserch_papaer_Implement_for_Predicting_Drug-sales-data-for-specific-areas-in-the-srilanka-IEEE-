SLIDE 7
Methodology
Visual Emotion Recognition (FER)
Data: FER-2013 dataset (7 emotions: Angry, Disgust, Fear, Happy, Sad, Surprise, Neutral).
Preprocessing: Image normalization, resizing (64x64), and advanced augmentation (rotation, flips, shifts) to combat class imbalance.
Model: A custom Deep Convolutional Neural Network (CNN).
Architecture: 5 convolutional blocks with increasing filters (64 → 512), Batch Normalization, Dropout layers.
Performance: Achieved 70.63% test accuracy on the challenging FER-2013 benchmark.

SLIDE 8
Methodology
Speech
 Emotion Recognition (SER)
Data: Combined CREMA-D & RAVDESS datasets for diversity and robustness.
Feature Extraction: MFCCs, Mel-Spectrograms, Spectral Contrast.
Model: A Hybrid CNN-LSTM architecture.
CNN: Extracts spatial features from audio spectrograms.
LSTM: Captures temporal dependencies in speech.
Performance: Achieved ~78.73% test accuracy on a refined set of 6 emotions, significantly improving upon baseline models.

modal Fusion & Agentic Recommendation

SLIDE 16
Result
Regarding fusion technology
Finding 1: Early testing with a limited, private dataset of 120 samples resulted in 41.67% accuracy, underscoring the dual challenges of data volume and audio-visual alignment.
Finding 2: A strategic shift to a curated, high-quality dataset—built from the SAVEE corpus and manually aligned images—yielded a dramatic performance increase to 74.67% accuracy.
Conclusion: This proves that dataset quality, size, and modality alignment are even more critical than model architecture for successful multimodal fusion. Our curated SAVEE-based dataset serves as a strong foundation for future work.
